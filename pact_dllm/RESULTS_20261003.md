## Engineering correction and current conclusion

After batching tile pooling, a CPU call-count diagnostic identified a second
problem: `ledger.dirty()` was recomputed for every known position. A2080-position
synthetic check made2048whole-canvas scans. `9cc1b53` computes the set once;
the same diagnostic now makes one scan. A regression check prevents reintroducing
this quadratic cost. CPU microbenchmarks are not GPU speed claims. The final
fixed-source GPU timing is still outstanding because of the idle guard above.

The architecture's safety semantics run on the real model, but the first proxy
does not yet demonstrate a strong joint-method advantage. Do not automatically
expand into128/task quality selection or claim superiority to Flash. A future
fixed candidate must be compared with the existing strong baselines under
uniform hardware/model/scoring, and independently validated at256/512lengths;
development128is separate from held-out offsets>=128. The original research
target remains unachieved.
